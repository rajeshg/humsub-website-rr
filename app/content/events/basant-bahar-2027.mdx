## Theme: Naad – Sounds of Nature

Welcome to **Naad – Sounds of Nature**, our theme for Basant Bahar 2027. What better way to welcome the advent of Spring, or Basant, than by connecting to the songs of the indigenous communities of India whose lives intertwine with the spirit of the lands they occupy. Naad speaks to the elemental bonds that join us to our roots and as the magic of spring unfolds, we invite you to revel in the sounds of those whose intimate relationship with nature finds expression in exuberant music, dance, and storytelling!

## About Naad

**Naad (नाद)** means sound — the primordial vibration at the heart of creation. In Indian tradition, Naad is the cosmic sound from which music, language and the universe itself are said to arise.

This theme celebrates the indigenous communities of India and their intimate bond with nature — songs, dances and stories passed down through generations, rooted in the lands they call home. As spring unfolds, we honor voices that have kept this connection alive through exuberant music, dance, and storytelling.
